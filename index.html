Home Robot Cell: a VLM-planned cell designed for precision

Vision-language models and learned policies plan and act at a few hertz, typically with centimeter-level error. Precise tasks need millimeters. This self-funded cell, built on personal time and equipment, is where I work on closing that gap. The target task is automated PCBA: aligning a through-hole component's pins over the board and inserting them.

A UFactory xArm Lite 6 with GELLO teleoperation, a fixed Basler camera and a wrist RealSense D435i run on one 16 GB GPU. Molmo2-ER acts as System 2: it plans and points, but never commands the arm. A behavior-tree System 1 handles precision, timing and safety checks; controller limits and the e-stop sit below it.

Each major part (vision-language model, policy, planner, detector) sits behind a defined interface, so it can be swapped as new models come out. Stack: ROS 2, ZMQ, Docker, Drake QP, LeRobot ACT, Isaac Sim, py_trees.
